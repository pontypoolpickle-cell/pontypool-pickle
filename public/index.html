Who We Are

The Pontypool Pickle Club is more than just a place to play—it's a community built on the historic spirit of our town. We are proud to represent Pontypool and welcome adults of all skill levels.

We encourage you to play with us and become part of the club’s community. Whether you live within Pontypool or live outside the area, you are equally welcome.

Payments & Membership

Event Payments

Event fees are paid instantly from your club wallet balance and must be settled within 15 minutes of booking your spot. Your place is only held for that 15-minute window - if payment isn't completed in time, the spot is automatically released and reassigned to the next reserve player, with club members receiving automatic priority. Top up your wallet balance any time using the 💰 Wallet button once logged in.

Cancellations & Refunds

We operate a 24-hour refund notice period: cancellations made 24 hours or more before the event starts are eligible for a refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the event starting are not eligible for a refund, regardless of whether a reserve player is found to take your spot.

Become a Member

Members get priority access to event signups, discounted pricing, and more. Head over to the Membership page to see pricing and perks.
